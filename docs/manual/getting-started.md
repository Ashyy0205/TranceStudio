# Getting started

Trance Studio builds a session from three kinds of material: themes (your images, videos and phrases),
looks (how they are shown) and audio. You arrange all three on the timeline, watch the result in the
viewer, and export it as a video when you are happy with it.

## Your first session

1. On the start screen, choose New session. The editor opens with an empty session and one look block.
2. Open the Media page (the Media tab at the top, or Ctrl+1). Add the folders where you keep your images
   and videos under Sources. They stay listed for every session.
3. Select some images or videos in the grid and drag them onto a theme on the right, or press Enter to
   add them to the highlighted theme. Use "Drop here for a new theme" to make a new one.
4. Go back to the Edit page (Ctrl+2). Select a theme in the Themes panel on the left and type a few
   phrases into its Phrases box, one per line.
5. Drag the theme from the Themes panel onto the Themes row of the timeline. Drag its edges to set when it
   starts and stops.
6. Drop an audio file onto the Foreground or Background row of the timeline.
7. Click the look block on the Looks row and choose which visual styles it uses, how fast it runs and its
   colours. "Apply a preset" is a quick way to start.
8. Press Space to play in the viewer, or F5 to watch fullscreen.
9. Save with Ctrl+S, and export a video with Ctrl+E when you are ready.

You can also drop a folder onto the start screen. A new session opens with one theme for each subfolder.

## How the pieces fit together

The timeline has independent rows. Nothing on one row is tied to anything on another, so a voice track
can run across several looks, and a theme can start halfway through a look.

The Looks row decides how things are shown: which visual styles appear, the speed, and the spiral and
text colours. See [Looks and visual styles](looks.md).

The Themes row decides what is shown: which images, videos and phrases. See
[Media and themes](media-and-themes.md).

The audio rows play your voice track, music and other sounds. See [Audio](audio.md).

The session ends when the last item on any row ends. See [The timeline](timeline.md).

## Getting help in the app

The first session tutorial walks you through the steps above with your own media. Each step waits until you
have done it, and at the end you have a saved session and a video. Start it from the Getting started
checklist at the top of the editor, or from the bottom of this manual's list of topics.

The checklist also ticks off each step when you do it on your own, and its Show me buttons point out where
to go. Hover over any button for a short description and its shortcut, and press F1 to open this manual at
the topic for whatever you have selected.
