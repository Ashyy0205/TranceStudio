# Watching and exporting

## The viewer

The viewer on the Edit page plays the session as it will look when exported. Press Space or click the
picture to play and pause. Edits apply while it plays. The look playing at the playhead is shown at the top
right of the viewer.

## Fullscreen

Press F5, or click Fullscreen, to watch the session full screen. Space pauses, and Esc returns to the
editor. The first time, you are asked to confirm a warning: sessions contain flashing images and spinning
patterns that can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.

## Exporting a video

Press Ctrl+E, or click Export, to save the session as an MP4 video with H.264 video and AAC audio.

Resolution can be 720p, 1080p, 1440p or 4K, or vertical 1080x1920 or square 1080 for phones and social
sites. The frame rate can be 30 or 60 fps. Quality trades export time against file size: Fast is quickest
and makes larger files, High is slowest and looks best.

GPU encoding uses your graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel) when it can and is several times faster. Turn it
off if an export fails or looks wrong.

The export runs at whatever speed your computer allows, which is usually faster than real time. You can
cancel it at any point.

## Rendering from the command line

Sessions can also be rendered without opening the editor. See the README for the options.
