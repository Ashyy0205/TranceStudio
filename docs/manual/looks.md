# Looks and visual styles

A look decides how a theme's media and phrases are shown: which visual styles appear, how fast everything
runs, and the colours of the spiral and text. Select a look block on the Looks row to change its look in
the inspector.

## Presets

"Apply a preset" sets the visual styles and speed in one step.

- Classic uses every visual style at the original speed.
- Gentle uses slower fades and steady images, which suits an induction.
- Intense uses accelerating, rapid-fire images and text.
- Text heavy puts your phrases front and centre.
- Video heavy favours videos and GIFs.

The colour swatches under Colours set the spiral and text colours in the same way. You can change anything
afterwards.

## Visual styles

The player switches between the styles that are turned on. The number next to a style is how often it is
picked compared with the others: a style set to 2 comes up about twice as often as one set to 1.

- Accelerate shows images that change faster and faster, with text over them.
- Slow flash alternates slow images and videos with bursts of rapid-fire images.
- Subtext shows changing images with faint walls of text behind them.
- Flash text fades smoothly between images, videos and text.
- Simple shows steady images with text over them.
- Triple layers three images or videos at once.
- Video shows videos with text over them.
- Super fast splices rapid images and text with brief video clips.

When a look block starts and the style on screen is not turned on in it, the player switches style straight
away.

## Motion

Speed is how fast everything runs. 100% is the speed of the original trance. Zoom is how strongly images
zoom towards you.

## Colours

Spiral A and Spiral B are the two colours of the spiral. The slider under each sets its opacity; lower
values make the spiral fainter. Reverse spiral direction turns it the other way.

Text is the colour of the phrases. Text shadow and subtext is the colour of the shadow behind phrases and of
the faint text in the Subtext style.

## Session settings

With nothing selected, the inspector shows settings for the whole session. Images in memory is how many
images are kept loaded at once; more gives more variety but uses more memory. Zoom depth is how far images
and the spiral appear to recede.
