# Media and themes

A theme is a set of images, videos, phrases and fonts that are shown together. A session can have as
many themes as you like. The Themes row of the timeline decides which theme plays when.

## What a theme holds

Images can be JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF or BMP files. Large images are scaled down when they load, so very big
files are fine.

Videos can be MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV or M4V files, or animated GIFs. The first time a video is used, its frames
are extracted in the background and the viewer shows "Preparing videos" until they are ready. This only
happens once per file; the frames are kept in a cache.

Phrases are the words that flash on screen. Type one phrase per line. A "/" in a phrase splits it over two
rows, so "Let go / and relax" shows as two lines. Import .txt adds the lines of a text file.
Make classic style turns every phrase to upper case and splits long ones over two rows, like the original
trance.

Fonts are TTF, OTF, WOFF or WOFF2 files used for that theme's phrases. A theme without fonts uses the
built-in font.

## The Media page

The Media page is for browsing large folders and picking what goes into each theme.

Sources, on the left, are the folders where you keep your media. They stay listed for every session.
Click a folder to show what is in it. Turn on "Include subfolders" to see everything below it at once.

The grid shows thumbnails. Click to select, Ctrl-click and Shift-click to select more, and Ctrl+A to
select everything shown. Double-click to see an item full size; in that view the arrow keys move through
the folder and Space selects.

The filters at the top show only images or only videos, hide items that are already in a theme, or
match a name.

The Themes list on the right is where selected media goes. Click a theme to make it the target, then press
Enter or click "Add to" to add the selection. You can also drag the selection onto any theme, or onto
"Drop here for a new theme". Items already in the target theme have a tick; with them selected, Delete
removes them from the theme. Your files are never deleted.

## The Themes panel

On the Edit page, the Themes panel on the left lists the session's themes. Click one to edit it in the
inspector: its name, phrases, images, videos and fonts.

The folder button adds a folder as themes, one for each subfolder. You can also drop folders or files onto
the panel, or onto a theme card to add them to that theme. "Not on the timeline" means the theme has no
block on the Themes row yet, so it will only appear in gaps.

Drag a theme card onto the Themes row of the timeline to schedule it. See [The timeline](timeline.md).
