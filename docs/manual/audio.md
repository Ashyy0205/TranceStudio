# Audio

Audio goes on two channels. A1 Foreground is meant for the voice track and A2 Background for music and
ambient sound, but you can use them however you like.

## Adding audio

Drop audio files onto a channel row at the point where they should start, or click "Add audio or drop files
here" on an empty row. Several files dropped together are placed one after another. Select a channel by
clicking its name and use "Add audio to this channel" to add files after its last clip. MP3, WAV, OGG,
FLAC, M4A, AAC, Opus and AIFF files work.

## Clips

A clip always plays in full, so its length cannot be changed. Drag a clip to move it, or drag it up or down
to move it to the other channel. Its edges snap to other blocks, clips and the playhead; hold Alt to place
it freely.

Clips on the same channel can overlap. Overlapping clips stack into extra lanes and play together.

Select a clip to set its start time, volume (up to 200%) and fade in and fade out. Jump to start moves the
playhead to the start of the clip.

## Channels

Each channel has a volume fader and an M button that mutes it. Double-click the fader to reset it to 100%.
Click the channel name to rename it or add files to it. A clip's final volume is its own volume multiplied
by its channel's volume. Muted channels are left out of the preview and the export.
